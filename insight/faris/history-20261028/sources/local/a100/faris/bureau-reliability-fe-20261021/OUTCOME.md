The proposed persistent customer/provider random effect was not supported by its target-free diagnostic. All three variance components were truncated to zero in every context-only fold; unconstrained moments and early/late provider-contrast correlations are retained in BUILD_COMPLETE.json. Correlations were near zero. This does not certify independent reporting errors, but does not justify this proposed persistent-error correction.

No classification fit was admitted. Fold-local candidate features and transformers remain retained for provenance; they are not a scored improvement or an extra completed pipeline. No default labels or public results were used for this decision.
