## H1: independent successful architectures
Our trimmed tabular model and friend's Kumo-plus-spline/offset use different learning mechanisms. Train our exact five recipes on friend's fixed five-fold assignments, preserving their original budgets. Test the preregistered 50:50 probability average against BOTH complete anchors; the 50:50 logit mean is a separate fixed sensitivity contrast. Neither weight is tuned on OOF.
Reject if pooled and matched-fold stability do not improve. The 56k fitted champion is separate from its 50k context control; do not hide the sample-cost effect of honest stacking.

## H2: correction for conditional underwriting information
APR surprise is observed APR minus a train-context-only nuisance regressor using permitted covariates and no default labels. A small spline offset on APR, expectation, residual and six financial reliability fields is fit only on 6k correction rows, with fixed L2=.03, inference strength=.25, maximum logit change=.5. ## H3: constrained useful diversity
Three context-trained specialists: account spline, bureau logistic and shopping XGBoost. Same context/correction/evaluation boundary. A convex mixture is learned only on correction rows, anchored with >=30% Kumo and >=30% champion, each specialist <=10%, ridge toward the equal anchor mixture. A separate smooth gate learns only how coverage and anchor disagreement modify the two-anchor weight; weights remain between20% and80%, L2=.03. Compare both to their exact context-equal control. No hard routing, broad hyperparameter tuning or global-OOF stacking.
